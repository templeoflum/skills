# Stages

**Draft, not a rule until Hedawn formalizes it.** Use it as guidance; don't hold work or workers to it.

Creative work climbs the standard animation pipeline. Nothing goes above its stage, and each step up needs the artist's Works on the one below.

## The two stages

- **Concept** (the default): roughs only. Previs or animatic, then blocking: Blender Workbench or viewport playblasts, flat shading, no textures, materials, lighting, motion blur or depth of field, at 540p or less. One version per round.
- **Production**: set only after the artist's Works on the concept. Polish, then look-dev and lighting, then the final render and comp.

Every round is ready for the artist the moment it exists, roughs included. Variants, such as other aspect ratios, are copied from a version after its Works, never made alongside it.

## The render ladder

One rung per Works, in standard order (previz → blocking → look → final):

1. **Concept:** viewport playblast or Workbench/EEVEE, quarter to half size, every other frame. Minutes, not hours. Stills or an animatic are fine.
2. **Motion approved:** EEVEE at half size, full frame rate, to judge timing and camera.
3. **Look approved:** a few Cycles stills of key frames for look, never the whole loop.
4. **Production:** the full Cycles render, once.

Cycles path tracing on a whole loop before rung 4 is a defect.

## Briefing a crew

Name the stage and the rung in the brief. Carry every rule from earlier rounds, not just the last note.
